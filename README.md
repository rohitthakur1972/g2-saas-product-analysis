# G2 SaaS Product Experience Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring customer reviews of SaaS products listed on G2. The objective was to evaluate customer satisfaction, compare product performance, identify recurring product strengths and weaknesses, and uncover actionable business insights from customer feedback.

The project combines Python-based data analysis, structured review metrics, and Excel dashboards to examine how customers perceive different SaaS products.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall customer satisfaction across SaaS products.
- Compare product performance using average ratings and review distributions.
- Identify frequently mentioned product features and customer pain points.
- Analyse positive and negative customer feedback patterns.
- Generate actionable insights to support product and business decision-making.

## Key Business Findings

**1. Product Performance**

- Awardco recorded the highest average rating among eligible products at 4.88/5.
- Smartsheet recorded the lowest average rating among eligible products at 3.62/5.
- Product ratings were compared to understand differences in customer satisfaction.

**2. Product Feature Analysis**

- Ease of use was the most frequently detected feature candidate, appearing in 34,673 reviews.
- Feature-level analysis helped identify recurring themes in customer feedback.

**3. Customer Pain Points**

- Pricing was the highest-count structured-dislike candidate, with 2,686 mentions.
- Customer feedback was examined to identify areas where SaaS products could improve.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Prepared and structured the review dataset for analysis.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined review distributions, product ratings, and customer feedback patterns.
3. **Product Performance Analysis:** Compared products using rating-based metrics.
4. **Feature Analysis:** Identified frequently discussed product features and customer pain points.
5. **Business Insights:** Consolidated analytical findings into structured reports.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Created Excel dashboards to communicate findings through visual reporting.
